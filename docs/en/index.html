A notebook for your game ideas.

Your notes, your moodboard and your mind map in a single window — with an assistant that reads your pages without ever sending them anywhere. It suggests, you decide.

Download for Windows View the code

Windows 10 and 11 · free · no account · 3.5 MB · MIT licence · interface in French

What it does

Built for the stage where nothing is settled yet: throw ideas in first, tidy up later.

Notes that nest

Pages inside pages, formatted text, tables, images, audio clips, and mentions from one page to another.

An endless moodboard

Drop images and videos on an infinite canvas, crop them, compare them. Paste a TikTok link: its thumbnail lands there, and the video plays in one click.

A living mind map

Your project at the centre, your pages around it. Link two pages with a coloured line and a label — or ask the AI which links it sees.

An assistant that stays home

It reads your pages to answer, suggests characters or quests, writes at the cursor. Nothing changes without your approval.

Find it, keep it

Search across every page with Ctrl+P, an automatic backup on every launch, and export a whole project as a .zip.

Your colours

Seven backgrounds, nine accent colours, and your own colours for the links on the map. Dark by default, light if you prefer.

Install

  1. Download the installer from the releases page, then run it.
  2. Windows will say “Unknown publisher”. Click More info, then Run anyway.
  3. That is all. It installs for your user only, no administrator rights needed.

Why that warning?

Windows trusts software signed with a certificate, which costs several hundred euros a year. Projekt is free and has none. In exchange, all the code is public: read it, and build it yourself if you would rather.

AI, on your own machine

Projekt works fine without it. If you want it, it runs on your computer, for free: no subscription, no API key, none of your pages sent over the internet.

  1. Install Ollama, which runs the models locally.
  2. In a terminal: ollama pull qwen3:8b for text, and ollama pull gemma3:4b to analyse images.
  3. Restart Projekt: the assistant appears at the bottom right (Ctrl+J).

A graphics card with 6 GB or more is recommended. Without one, answers will simply take longer.

Your data stays yours

Everything lives in a folder on your computer: one database and your files. No account, no server, no analytics. A backup is made automatically on every launch, and any project can be exported as a single .zip.

Two features reach the internet, and only if you use them: the assistant’s Wikipedia lookup, which is off by default, and moodboard TikTok videos, which play from TikTok.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes, with no paid tier hiding behind it. The app is released under the MIT licence.

Mac or Linux?

Not for now: Projekt was built on Windows and has only been tested there.

Is the app in English?

Not yet — the interface is in French. Translating it is on the list.

Can I take my projects elsewhere?

Yes. The .zip export holds a file you can re-import, and also your pages as Markdown, readable in any text editor.

Can the AI change my pages on its own?

No. It prepares a proposal, you see exactly what it would change, and nothing is written until you click.
